04 Transparent by design

What the score
actually means.

The match score combines role alignment (30%), CV skill overlap (40%), seniority (15%), Hamburg/remote fit (10%), and domain relevance (5%). It is a prioritization aid, not a hiring probability.

How salary estimates work

Employer-published ranges pass through unchanged. Otherwise the model starts from Bundesagentur für Arbeit medians, applies a role-family and level factor, then a small profile-fit adjustment. The headline anchor never moves below Entgeltatlas and rises only with at least three comparable published Data Scientist ranges. Estimated ranges are intentionally broad and shown with medium or low confidence.

Where jobs come from

The daily updater visits permitted official career pages, refreshes Experimentation Jobs and the community-documented BA Jobsuche endpoint, and uses at most ten Brave Search requests per UTC day. A stated application deadline retires a role immediately after it passes; otherwise a role moves to the archive after 60 days without confirmation. Only job-specific application pages are retained. Google Search, LinkedIn, and Bundesagentur web pages are not scraped.

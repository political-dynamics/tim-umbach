05 / Method & limitations

A benchmark you can question.

“Fair market price” here means a central asking-price benchmark for comparable apartments. The model has no transaction history, occupancy, cancellations, or revenue data and has not been backtested against completed bookings.

Nearby, with room for the same guests

Brave Search discovers sources, supplemented by a walk through the full official island directory. The collector extracts apartment-level prices, floor area, capacity, and coordinates directly from public amrum.de records. The map can show the whole island; the pricing model uses Nebel comparables. Alte Schule is excluded from the market sample. Whole houses, incomplete records, and flats outside 20–100 m² are excluded. Selected peers must fit the target’s guest capacity (at most two extra places) and be between half and twice its floor area.

Size adjustment, without false precision

Up to seven nearest properties contribute, with one apartment per property. Distance uses log floor-area difference, capacity difference (0.18 per guest), and bedroom difference when known (0.15 per room). The estimate is the weighted median of asking prices multiplied by the square root of target area / comparable area. Weights are exp(−2 × distance). These coefficients are assumptions, not fitted effects.

A modest seasonal evidence base

The September 2026 portal prices are treated as B-season proxies; “from” prices do not guarantee availability or a fully comparable fee basis. A and C factors are medians of provider-level tariff ratios from two independent owners. One owner’s tariff is undated. Christmas uses a separate factor because the owners assign it to different seasons. The target apartments’ own prices never determine these factors.

What the range means

The band includes the weighted 20th–80th percentile of adjusted peer prices, the spread of the two owners’ seasonal factors, and at least ±20% around the unrounded estimate. Prices round to €5. This is a sensitivity range, with no claimed coverage probability. Small four-person flats require particularly substantial size extrapolation.

Fees and the next year

Known additional cleaning fees are spread over seven nights; an unknown fee is left unfilled, not treated as evidence that cleaning is free. Linen, service, minimum stays, quality, view, and amenities may differ. amrum.sh says linen and service are included. Visitor tax is excluded everywhere. The 2027 model holds the observed 2026 market level constant; its calendar and the owner’s published 2027 tariffs update separately.

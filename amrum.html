06 / Method & limitations

A benchmark you can question.

“Fair market price” here means a central asking-price benchmark for comparable apartments. The model has no transaction history, occupancy, cancellations, or revenue data and has not been backtested against completed bookings.

All eligible island apartments

The collector extracts prices, floor area, capacity, bedrooms and location from public amrum.de records. All eligible apartments enter the regression, with no seven-property cap. Alte Schule, including any listings at its address, is excluded. Whole houses, incomplete records, and flats outside 20–100 m² remain excluded. Nearby examples help inspect the result; they do not limit the model sample.

Fitted size, capacity and location effects

A ridge regression predicts log nightly asking price from log floor area, guest capacity, bedrooms, a missing-bedroom indicator and village indicators. Each building has equal total weight, shared across its apartments. Regularization is chosen from four strengths using five validation folds that keep buildings intact. Estimates are conditional central prices on the log scale, rounded to €5.

Off-season is included; evidence is thinner

January–February and late October–December use the off-season tariff factor. The September 2026 portal “from” prices are assumed to represent the shoulder-season price level; they are not date-specific quotes. Seasonal factors come from 12 apartments at five independent owners, each owner weighted equally. One schedule is undated. Christmas has a separate factor. More portal records improve the fitted price level but do not add seasonal observations. The target’s tariffs never enter the model.

Does a year adjustment help?

What the range means

The range uses the building-weighted 10th–90th percentiles of held-out log-price errors, widened by the lowest and highest provider seasonal factors. It describes variation in asking prices, not uncertainty in the mean or guaranteed coverage for bookings. Seasonal, fee and portal-price assumptions are not validated by the regression check.

Fees and the next year

Known additional cleaning fees are spread over seven nights; an unknown fee is left unfilled, not treated as evidence that cleaning is free. Linen, service, minimum stays, quality, view, and amenities may differ. amrum.sh says linen and service are included. Visitor tax is excluded everywhere. The 2027 model holds the observed 2026 market level constant; its calendar and the owner’s published 2027 tariffs update separately.

I help product teams decide what to launch, improve, or investigate next—using rigorous experiments when possible and pragmatic causal analysis when the data is messy.

Let’s work together ↗

02 / Profile

Evidence that holds up beyond the notebook.

I start with the decision a team needs to make, then translate it into a measurable hypothesis, primary outcome, guardrails, and a design that can produce a credible answer. That includes sample-size planning, instrumentation checks, randomisation diagnostics, effect estimation, and sensitivity analysis—not only the final significance test.

The result is a recommendation with magnitude, uncertainty, trade-offs, and clearly stated assumptions. I build the underlying workflows in Python, SQL, and Google Cloud, and communicate the evidence in language that product, design, marketing, and leadership teams can act on.

03 / Expertise

Where I add value.

01

Experimentation

I take tests from an initial product question to a decision-ready readout: hypotheses, power and MDE planning, KPI and guardrail design, quality checks, effect estimation, and corrections when many outcomes are evaluated.

02

Causal inference

When randomisation is unavailable, I make the identification strategy explicit and use methods such as difference-in-differences, instrumental variables, panel models, and time-series designs to separate signal from plausible alternatives.

03

Product analytics

I connect clickstream, transaction, and operational data to product behaviour, define KPIs that reflect real user and business outcomes, and turn recurring questions into reusable analytical workflows rather than one-off reports.

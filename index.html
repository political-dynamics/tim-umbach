02 / Profile

Reliable evidence for real product questions.

I design and analyse experiments end to end—from hypotheses, KPIs, and power calculations through effect estimation and business interpretation. When clean experiments are not possible, I use robust statistical and causal methods to get as close to a trustworthy answer as the data allows.

My work combines hands-on analysis in Python, SQL, and Google Cloud with clear guidance for product, design, marketing, and leadership teams.
